Add 8 icons, refine the artwork of 57 icons, and give the `-alt` icons descriptive names.

**New icons.** `agent-circle`, `agent-clover`, `agent-flower`, `agent-triangle`, `chart-line-dots`, `equals`, `menu-simple` and `password-cursor`.

**Refined icons.** Including the `calendar-*`, `camera-*`, `message-square-*`, `mic-*` and `signal-*` icons, `arrow-corner-*`, `layers`, `minus`, `server`, `tv` and `upload`.

**Renamed icons.** These icons now say how they differ instead of ending in `-alt`. The old names keep working as deprecated aliases until 9.0.0:

- `asterisk-alt` → `asterisk-serif`
- `chart-candle-alt` → `chart-candle-3`
- `chart-pie-alt` → `chart-pie-3`
